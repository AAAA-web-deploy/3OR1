# Research: meme websites for $3OR1 design benchmarking

Research date: **October 9, 2026**. Sources are public websites and the user's supplied references, not other private chats. Visual specifics may change as sites update.

## Selected primary benchmark: Wojak CTO

**https://www.wojakcto.com/**

Observed public site structure includes a brand-forward landing area, a directly navigable **“Meme Gen”** item, meme character images, a **“Many Faces of Wojak”** gallery, social CTAs, and beginner how-to-buy content. What makes it especially applicable is the notion that a meme character has multiple repeatable forms and the generator is a primary destination—not just a side graphic.

### Patterns to adapt to $3OR1

- **Character-first landing:** recognizable mascot before extensive copy.
- **Generator prominent in nav:** the Fusion Lab should be visible one click from entry.
- **Face variations are the content:** 15 original emotion sprites and 10 prepared outcomes are a reason to explore.
- **Distinct scroll chapters:** a short visually sequenced story before the interactive lab.
- **Low-friction CTAs:** obvious but honest buy/join actions.
- **Meme-heavy composition:** oversized art, bright panels, expressive short text, mobile simplicity.

### How we distinguish $3OR1

- A sequential, lore-specific **Raj + Anatoly/Solana → Vitalik/Ethereum → question-mark fusion** story.
- Three synchronized expression selectors and a big result stage rather than one generic meme generator.
- Five exact same-emotion artworks, five featured mixed examples, plus interactive Canvas collage for other choices.
- An **open question** (“One mind—or Three?!”), not an assertion that every fusion works.
- Own neon/blue/magenta/gold palette anchored in the user's actual banner, not Wojak's image files or HTML.

## Secondary references reviewed

**https://www.pepe.vip/**: concise meme-first landing, large central character imagery, straight-forward sections for buying and tokenomics. Good reminder to keep explanatory copy sparse. We deliberately do **not** copy claimed tokenomics from it.

**https://froge.vip/**: story-led homepage, game/leaderboard references, modern section hierarchy and community destinations. Good lesson: one interactive feature can make a meme site feel alive. Do not imply our site has a leaderboard or live wallet feature when it doesn't.

**https://apu.io/**: extensive meme and music culture, demonstrating that a repeatable character can become a large community content library. Good gallery inspiration; deliberately avoid copying its media.

## Original design direction delivered

Dark-neon cyber-comic palette; bright cobalt, electric cyan, magenta, gold; distinctive gold-framed mascot; visual panels with punchy headings and very short copy. The source images in the user uploads drive the actual visual identity. The benchmark concerns organization, storytelling, effects, and participation, not duplication of copyright-protected source assets.
